Help & FAQ

Common questions about Australia Explorer.

What is Australia Explorer?

A community-driven map of real-time situation reports across Australia — weather, hazards, traffic, noise, and emergencies, posted by your neighbours and verified by your community.

How do I post a report?

Click Create Report in the sidebar, fill in the state, city, category, and a short description. You can attach up to 5 images or videos.

What are the categories?

Five categories: ☁️ Weather, 🔊 Noisiness, ⚠️ Hazards, 🚦 Traffic, and 🚨 Emergency. Pick whichever best matches what you're reporting.

What are favourites?

Cities and reports you've starred ⭐ to keep an eye on. View them anytime from the Favourites link in the sidebar.

How do trust scores work?

Each verified report you post adds to your trust score. Higher trust scores indicate users whose reports are most often confirmed by others.

Can I browse without an account?

Yes — click Continue as guest on the home page. Guests can view the map and the reports listing, but need to sign up to post or save favourites.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on our GitHub repo, or message a project member directly. We'll triage and fix as quickly as we can.
