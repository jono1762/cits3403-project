Help & FAQ

Common questions about Australia Explorer.

What is Australia Explorer?

A community-driven map of real-time situation reports across Australia — weather, hazards, traffic, noise, and emergencies, posted by your neighbours and verified by your community.

For everyone (no account needed)

Search a city on the map

Open the Map page, type a city into the search bar, click the pin, and you'll see options to view live weather, browse reports for that city, or save it as a favourite (saving requires an account).

See what's trending across Australia

On the map page, the top stats show the trending city (the city with the most reports in the current trending top-10), the trending category (most-reported category in the trending top-10), and from your saves (the highest-scoring report across your saved cities and reports — logged-in only).

Browse and filter reports

Click View Reports to see every active report. Filter by state, city, sort by Latest / Most verifications / Most disputes / Most comments, and toggle the Trending or From Following feeds. Hit Clear to reset all filters.

Check live weather

Open the Weather page and pick a city to see current temperature, conditions, wind, and precipitation. Data refreshes from Open-Meteo.

Look at someone's profile

Use the search bar in the navbar (top of the page) to find users by name. Their profile shows their reports, their credibility score, and their follower / following counts.

After you log in

Create a report

Click Create Report in the sidebar. Pick a state, city, category, write a short description, and attach up to 5 images or videos (max 200 MB total).

Verify, dispute, or save a report

On any report card you don't own, tap ✓ Verify if you can confirm it, or ✗ Dispute if you can't. Tap the ⭐ to save the report to your favourites for later.

Save favourite cities and reports

Click Favourites in the sidebar to see your saved locations and saved reports. Save a city from its map pin popup, and save a report from its card star button.

Edit your profile picture and bio

Open Profile from the navbar dropdown and click Edit profile. You can upload a new avatar (image preview included) and update your short bio.

Follow and unfollow other users

On someone's profile page, click Follow to subscribe to their reports. They'll appear in your From Following feed. Click again to unfollow.

Check your credibility and your follower / following counts

Your credibility score rises with each report of yours that others verify. The follower / following counters on your profile are clickable to see the actual lists (assuming you've left them public — see below).

Hide your follower or following list

On your own profile, each list has a small toggle that flips it between public and private. Private means other users see the count but can't open the list.

Message another user

From someone's profile click Message, or open the Messages icon in the navbar. You can send text, images, and videos. New conversations from people you don't follow back land in your Requests tab until you reply or accept.

Block or unblock a user

Inside any chat, click the Block button next to that user's name to stop further messages from them — they can't reach you and you can't reach them until you unblock. To see and unblock everyone you've blocked, click the small blocked-users icon next to the "Messages" title at the top of the Messages page.

More

What are the categories?

Five categories: ☁️ Weather, 🔊 Noisiness, ⚠️ Hazards, 🚦 Traffic, and 🚨 Emergency. Pick whichever best matches what you're reporting.

How does credibility work?

Each verified report you post adds to your credibility. Higher credibility indicates users whose reports are most often confirmed by others.

Can I browse without an account?

Yes — click Continue as guest on the home page. Guests can view the map and the reports listing, but need to sign up to post, vote, save favourites, or message users.
