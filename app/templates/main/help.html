Help & FAQ

Common questions about Australia Explorer.

What is Australia Explorer?

A community-driven map of real-time situation reports across Australia: weather, hazards, traffic, noise, and emergencies, posted by your neighbours and verified by your community.

Getting started

Sign up or log in

Click Log in on the home page to sign in with your username or email. New users can sign up from the same page. You can change your password later from Settings.

Return to the home page

Click the Australia Explorer logo at the top left of any page to jump back to the home page.

For everyone (no account needed)

Search a city on the map

Open the Map page, type a city into the search bar, click the pin, and you'll see options to view live weather, browse reports for that city, or save it as a favourite (saving requires an account).

See what's trending across Australia

On the map page, the top stats show the trending city (the city with the most reports in the current trending top-10), the trending category (most-reported category in the trending top-10), and from your saves (the highest-scoring report across your saved cities and reports). Each panel is clickable and takes you to the matching listing or report.

Browse and filter reports

Click View Reports to see every active report. Filter by state, city, sort by Latest / Most verifications / Most disputes / Most comments, and toggle the Trending or From Following feeds. Hit Clear to reset all filters.

View a single report and its comments

Click any report card to open its full view, where you'll see the description, attached media, and every comment. Click a comment to jump back to that same report view at the comment thread.

Check live weather

Open the Weather page and pick a city to see current temperature, conditions, wind, and precipitation. Data comes from Open-Meteo.

After you log in

Create a report

Click Create Report in the sidebar. Pick a state, city, category, write a short description, and attach up to 5 images or videos (200 MB total).

Verify, dispute, or save a report

On any report card you don't own, tap ✓ Verify if you can confirm it, or ✗ Dispute if you can't. Tap the star to save the report to your favourites for later.

Comment on a report (with media)

Inside a single-report view, scroll to the comment box. You can post a text comment and optionally attach images or videos. Other users can verify or dispute your comment too, just like a report. You can delete your own comments any time.

Edit or delete your own reports

On a report card you authored you'll see an Edit pill (top right) instead of the verify / dispute buttons. Open the report view to edit details, update media, or delete it entirely.

Save favourite cities and reports

Click Favourites in the sidebar to see your saved locations and saved reports. Save a city from its map pin popup, and save a report from its card star button.

Trending mechanics

What does the 🔥 Trending badge mean?

A report shows the 🔥 Trending badge when it's in the global top 10 by score. The badge appears on the reports listing, profile post cards, and favourite reports, so it always means the same thing wherever you see it.

How is trending scored?

Score is roughly verifies minus disputes minus days old: newer reports with lots of verifies rank highest. To stop fake-account gaming, votes from accounts less than 7 days old don't count toward the trending score. The verify / dispute totals shown on the card still include every vote, only the ranking ignores fresh accounts.

Why did a report disappear?

Every report has an expiry time. Once it expires it stops showing in the listing and on the map. Cards that are close to expiring show an "expiring soon" hint so you can verify or dispute before they vanish.

Profile and social

Edit your profile picture and bio

Open Profile from the navbar dropdown and click Edit profile. You can upload a new avatar (up to 4 MB) and update your short bio. Your profile also shows every report you've posted.

Follow and unfollow other users

On someone's profile page, click Follow to subscribe to their reports. They'll appear in your From Following feed. Click again to unfollow.

Check your credibility and your follower / following counts

Your credibility score rises with each report of yours that others verify. The follower / following counters on your profile are clickable to see the actual lists (assuming you've left them public, see below).

Hide your follower or following list

On your own profile, each list has a small toggle that flips it between public and private. Private means other users see the count but can't open the list.

Find another user's profile

Use the search bar in the navbar to find users by name. Their profile shows their reports, credibility score, and follower / following counts (when public).

Messaging

Message another user

From someone's profile click Message, or open the Messages icon in the navbar. You can send text, images, and videos. New conversations from people you don't follow back land in their Requests tab until they accept.

Block or unblock a user

Inside any chat, click the Block button next to that user's name to stop further messages from them. They can't reach you and you can't reach them until you unblock. To see and unblock everyone you've blocked, click the blocked-users icon next to the "Messages" title at the top of the Messages page.

Account settings

Change username, email, or password

Open Settings from the sidebar. You can update your username, email, and password. Password changes require your current password for safety.

Remove your avatar

In Settings, click Remove avatar to go back to the default letter avatar (the coloured circle with your initial).

Delete your account

Settings has a Delete account option. Deletion removes your profile, reports, comments, follows, and messages. This can't be undone.

More

What are the categories?

Five categories: ☁️ Weather, 🔊 Noisiness, ⚠️ Hazards, 🚦 Traffic, and 🚨 Emergency. Pick whichever best matches what you're reporting.

What can guests do?

Guests can browse the home page, map, reports listing, individual reports, live weather, this help page, and the about page. To verify, dispute, save, comment, create reports, follow, message, view other profiles, or use settings, you need to log in.
