About Australia Explorer

A community-driven map of real-time situation reports.

What we do

Australia Explorer surfaces real-time situation reports — weather, hazards, traffic, noisiness, and emergencies — across every state and territory. Locals post what they see, the community verifies it, and everyone benefits from a clearer picture of what's happening right now.
